## 1. Sector-level previews (the substantive reads)

### Garanti BBVA Yatırım — 2Q26 Earnings Preview (21 Jul 2026) 🟢 TR
- PDF: https://www.garantibbvayatirim.com.tr/medium/ResearchReports-Constant-83300.vsf (5 pp.)
- Press write-up: https://paraajansi.com.tr/haber/banka-hisseleri-icin-yeni-rapor-garanti-bbvadan-2c26-beklentileri-22228267h
- **The most quoted preview of the season.** Average **-29% q/q** net income across coverage; **~250bp**
  loan–deposit spread contraction → **~-45bp** average NIM; resilient fees vs seasonally higher opex;
  higher provisions, moderate CoR increase. Expects **most banks to cut 2026 NIM guidance**.
- Outperformers: **VakıfBank** (low effective tax, strong fees), **TSKB** (non-deposit funding = insulated,
  flat q/q profit). Laggards: **Yapı Kredi**, **Halkbank** (margin pressure + weak trading).
- Valuation anchor: sector at a **73% discount to EM peers** vs a 10-yr average discount of 55%.
  Model portfolio keeps **Akbank** and **Yapı Kredi**.

### İş Yatırım — 2. Çeyrek 2026 Bilanço Tahminleri (24 Jul 2026) 🟢 TR
- Landing: https://arastirma.isyatirim.com.tr/2026/07/24/2-ceyrek-2026-bilanco-tahminleri/
- PDF: https://arastirma.isyatirim.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2C2026_kar_tahminleri_TR-1.pdf
- Covered banks: **₺76.2bn** aggregate net profit, **-22% q/q / +13% y/y**. NIM compression + normalised
  trading gains, partly offset by strong fees and relatively limited provisioning. Positive outliers

### HSBC — Türkiye 2Q26 Earnings Preview (20 Jul 2026) 🔴 (report) / 🟢 (summary)

## Caveats

- **Bulge-bracket reports (HSBC, Goldman, JPM, Morgan Stanley, UBS, Barclays, Wood) are hard-paywalled.**
  What is linked above are free Turkish-press summaries of them — accurate on direction and headline
  numbers, unreliable on tables and target prices. Don't quote a bulge-bracket figure from a press summary
  in anything we publish.
- **Local broker PDFs are free but often unauthenticated deep links** that rotate (Garanti BBVA Yatırım's
  `ResearchReports-Constant-*.vsf` in particular). Copies of the two primary PDFs pulled on 2026-07-30
  were not committed — re-download from the links above.
- Neither PDF could be text-extracted in this environment (both are image-heavy); the numbers above come
  from the publishers' own landing pages and press write-ups, not from parsing the PDFs.
- Estimates quoted are **pre-results** for every bank that had not reported by 30 Jul 2026. Where a bank
  has reported, the actual is marked as such in §0.
